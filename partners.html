What Mainstreet does

Before someone buys a business, they need to know if the earnings they're paying for are real. Sellers need the same answer before they go to market. That's a Quality of Earnings review, and it's what we do. Mainstreet is led by a Chartered Professional Accountant licensed in Alberta, Canada, and serves clients across North America.

What you do

  • Find people buying or selling a business: owners getting ready to sell, search funders, first-time buyers, and the advisors around them.
  • Start a real conversation. LinkedIn, email, phone, your own network, online communities.
  • Register the prospect with us before you introduce them, so nobody gets contacted twice.
  • Once a prospect is interested, introduce them to our team and join the first meeting. We take it from there: scope, pricing and all of the work.

You don't need an accounting background. You need to understand what we offer and be good at starting conversations.

Who fits

  • Strong written and spoken English.
  • A LinkedIn profile. It's required once you join, because it's how you'll reach people.
  • At least 5 hours a week. Go full-time if you want to.
  • Students: this is built for you. Work from your laptop around classes, build real B2B sales experience and a track record with a financial due diligence firm.
  • Either a network of business owners, buyers or deal people, or real skill at personalized outreach. Students and early-career people are welcome.

Not eligible: employees, officers or agents of banks or lenders; people working at CPA, bookkeeping or fractional CFO firms; licensed or regulated professionals such as lawyers, business or real estate brokers, and financial or insurance advisors.

How it works

  1. Apply. About 2 minutes. Name, contact details and a few quick questions.
  2. 15-minute intro call. You pick a time yourself as soon as you submit. We cover how the role works and confirm it's a fit.
  3. Sign the partner agreement and complete a quick training on what we offer and how we work.
  4. Start. Register prospects, make introductions, earn.
