Have this ready

  • Whether you have a target business, and the industries and locations you're considering
  • Your purchase price range and credit score range
  • Approximate balances for cash, retirement accounts, marketable securities and home equity, plus any proposed investor funding
  • Whether all owners are U.S. citizens or U.S. nationals. Lendesca's tool asks this as part of SBA eligibility screening; other eligibility requirements also apply.
  • Your resume, which Lendesca reviews to assess experience

Why two firms

Lender underwriting assesses the financing request and repayment capacity. Mainstreet's financial diligence helps you understand the target's earnings, adjustments and financial risks from the buyer's perspective.

Each firm keeps its own role, and the participating lender makes every credit decision.
