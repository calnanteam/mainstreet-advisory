Show us you can run the outreach.

We give you the scripts and the targets. Round 2 checks one thing: can you take a real prospect, personalize our script, and get the message right. It takes about 20 minutes.

What we do

Mainstreet Advisory does quality of earnings reports (QoE) for people buying small businesses in the US and Canada. A QoE checks whether the seller's earnings hold up before the buyer pays for the business. Our founder, Matt Calnan, is a CPA. Flat fees, starting at $3,500. Most reports take 1 to 4 weeks.

Your job

Find business brokers, buyers and small-business lenders on LinkedIn, personalize our scripts, and book interested people onto a 15-minute call with Matt. Matt handles everything after the call.

Pay: $25 USD for every qualified first call that's held, plus $100 USD more if that prospect hires Mainstreet. Commission only. No fee to join. You may earn $0. Independent contractor.

The scripts

Give first, then ask. Personalize the brackets with something real about the person. Keep the rest. Don't add promises.

Script 1 · Connection note to a broker

"Hi [first name], saw [something real: a listing, a recent deal, a post]. I work with Mainstreet Advisory. We do fast, flat-fee quality of earnings reports for buyers on small deals, so diligence doesn't hold up your closings. Would love to connect."

Script 2 · Message after they connect (give first)

"Thanks for connecting, [first name]. We work with buyers who are actively looking for businesses, and we like sending them to good brokers. What are you listing right now? Happy to pass it along. We also have a free SBA buyer guide your buyers can use: mainstreetfirm.com/sba-buyer-guide. If one of them ever needs a quality of earnings report, we turn them around in 1 to 4 weeks, flat fee. Happy to set up a short call with Matt so you two can compare notes."

Script 3 · Buyers and searchers

"Hi [first name], saw you're looking to buy [type of business]. Before you close, a quality of earnings report shows whether the seller's numbers hold up. We do them flat fee from $3,500, usually in 1 to 4 weeks. Happy to set up a short call with our founder Matt if you want to talk it through."

Follow-up · once, about 5 days later

"Hi [first name], bumping this in case it got buried. If a QoE comes up on one of your deals, we'd love to help. No worries if not."

When someone asks: "Why pay for this if my accountant can look at the financials?"

"Fair question. Your accountant does the books and taxes. A QoE is the buyer's check on the numbers before they pay for the business: are the earnings real, what's one-off, what needs adjusting. Different job. Happy to set up a short call with Matt if you want to go deeper."

The rules

  • Always say you work with Mainstreet Advisory.
  • US prospects only for now. Canadian contacts go to Matt.
  • Check every name with us before you message them, so nobody hears from us twice.
  • Every message is personal and sent by hand. No bots, auto-senders or scraping. Using AI to help you draft is fine.
  • No promises about results, no advice. Questions go to Matt.
  • If someone says no or asks you to stop, stop and tell us the same day.
